# Email Template Selection Guide

A simple guide to help you pick the right template every time.

---

## Quick Decision Chart

```
Found a job posting online?
  --> Did you already apply?
        YES --> Did you get interview call?
                  YES --> Did interview happen?
                            YES --> Use: THANK YOU
                            NO  --> Use: INTERVIEW SCHEDULING
                  NO  --> Use: FOLLOW UP
        NO  --> Use: JOB APPLICATION

No job posting, but found company email on their website?
  --> Use: COLD OUTREACH

Someone you know works at the company?
  --> Use: REFERRAL REQUEST
```

---

## Template Details

### 1. JOB APPLICATION

**When to use:**
You found a specific job posting on a job portal (LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, company careers page)
and you are applying for it.

**Situation examples:**
- You saw "Data Scientist" opening on LinkedIn and want to apply
- A company posted "AI/ML Engineer" on Naukri and you want to send your resume
- You found a job listing on a company's careers page

**Subject line looks like:**
`Application for Data Scientist -- Shashwat | Ready for Immediate Joining`

**What it does:**
- Introduces you as a candidate for that specific role
- Lists what you bring (skills, track record, collaboration)
- Asks for a 15-minute call
- Shows you can join immediately

---

### 2. INTERVIEW SCHEDULING

**When to use:**
The company already replied to your application and wants to schedule an interview.
You need to send them your available time slots.

**Situation examples:**
- HR emailed back saying "We'd like to interview you"
- You got shortlisted and they asked for your availability
- Recruiter asked "When are you free for a call?"

**Subject line looks like:**
`Re: Interview Scheduling -- Data Scientist | Shashwat (Available This Week)`

**What it does:**
- Thanks them for shortlisting you
- Gives your available time slots (weekdays + Saturday)
- Shows flexibility to adjust to their schedule
- Attaches updated resume

---

### 3. FOLLOW UP

**When to use:**
You already applied for a job but haven't heard back in 5-7 days.
You want to politely remind them about your application.

**Situation examples:**
- Applied on LinkedIn 1 week ago, no response yet
- Sent resume to HR 10 days ago, silence
- Company said "We'll get back to you" but didn't

**Subject line looks like:**
`Following Up: Data Scientist Application -- Shashwat | Still Very Interested`

**What it does:**
- Politely reminds them about your application
- Recaps your value in bullet points (so they don't need to re-read resume)
- Asks for a quick call this week
- Re-attaches your resume

**Tip:** Don't send more than 2 follow-ups. First after 7 days, second after 14 days.

---

### 4. THANK YOU (Post-Interview)

**When to use:**
Your interview just happened (today or yesterday) and you want to leave
a strong final impression.

**Situation examples:**
- You just finished a phone/video interview
- You had an in-person interview and want to follow up same day
- Technical round is done and you want to stay top-of-mind

**Subject line looks like:**
`Thank You for the Data Scientist Interview -- Shashwat | Excited to Join`

**What it does:**
- Thanks them for their time
- Reinforces why you're the right fit (bullet points)
- Shows excitement about joining
- Keeps you fresh in their memory before they decide

**Tip:** Send within 24 hours of the interview for maximum impact.

---

### 5. REFERRAL REQUEST

**When to use:**
You know someone who works at the company (friend, ex-colleague, college senior)
and you want them to refer you internally for a role.

**Situation examples:**
- Your college senior works at Google and there's an opening
- Ex-colleague is at a company that's hiring for your role
- LinkedIn connection works at a company you want to join

**Subject line looks like:**
`Would You Refer Me? Data Scientist Role at Google -- Shashwat`

**What it does:**
- Makes a clear, polite request for referral
- Gives a quick snapshot of your skills (easy to forward to HR)
- Asks for even a brief introduction (low effort for them)
- Attaches resume so they can forward it directly

**Tip:** Make it easy for them -- this email is designed so they can
just forward it to their HR with your resume attached.

---

### 6. COLD OUTREACH

**When to use:**
You visited a company's website, liked what they do, found their HR/team email,
but there is NO specific job posting. You want to introduce yourself and
ask if they have any openings.

**Situation examples:**
- Found a startup's website, liked their work, found info@company.com or hr@company.com
- Saw a company on social media, no job posted, but you want to reach out
- Visiting company websites and collecting HR emails to send your resume
- No specific job listing exists but you want to be on their radar

**Subject line looks like:**
`Experienced Data Scientist -- Actively Seeking Opportunities at TechCorp | Shashwat`

**What it does:**
- Leads with your value (what you can do for them)
- Shows your skills in bullet points (scannable in 5 seconds)
- Asks for a quick 10-15 minute conversation
- Keeps the door open even if no current openings ("keep me in mind")

**Tip:** This is your most versatile template. When in doubt, use this one.

## Summary Table

| # | Template              | Use When...                                          | You Need...           |
|---|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | Job Application       | Applying to a specific job posting                   | Job title + HR email  |
| 2 | Interview Scheduling  | They want to interview you, need your availability   | HR email              |

## Typical Flow (How These Connect)

```
Step 1:  Found company email, no job posting?  -->  COLD OUTREACH
         Found specific job posting?            -->  JOB APPLICATION
         Know someone at the company?           -->  REFERRAL REQUEST

Step 2:  No reply after 7 days?                -->  FOLLOW UP

Step 3:  They want to interview?               -->  INTERVIEW SCHEDULING

Step 4:  Interview done?                        -->  THANK YOU
```

This is the full journey. You may not always go through all steps,
but this is the ideal flow for maximum chances of getting selected.
